## Описание
Промт для создания фотореалистичного объекта из фото внутри прозрачной стеклянной коробки.
Подходит для генерации сувенирных, коллекционных и product-style изображений.

## Prompt

photorealistic image of a 3D SUBJECT from image encased in a luxurious transparent box, viewed from an enhanced side angle to better reveal the 3D shape of the SUBJECT from image. The box should be white, exquisitely designed, featuring crystal-clear glass with refined, sharp edges

## Notes

Replace:
SUBJECT from image

with:
- person from photo
- object from photo
- character from photo
- toy from photo
- figurine from photo

Works best with:
- product lighting
- studio background
- photorealistic mode
