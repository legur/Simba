## Пост
Кинематографичный уличный портрет в толпе: герой стоит неподвижно и смотрит прямо в объектив, а поток людей вокруг превращается в длинную смазанную динамику.

## Инструкция
1. Открой генератор изображений с поддержкой image-to-image.
2. Загрузи исходное фото человека.
3. Вставь основной промпт.
4. При необходимости добавь negative prompt в отдельное поле или в конец запроса.

## Промпт
```text
Ultra-realistic cinematic street portrait of the person from the uploaded image standing still in a crowded city street, looking directly into the camera with a calm, intense expression. Sharp focus on the subject’s face while the crowd moves around him, creating a strong long-exposure motion blur with directional streaks. The subject remains perfectly still and crisp. Natural soft daylight, shallow depth of field, creamy bokeh, realistic skin texture, subtle freckles, neutral makeup, dark winter coat and scarf. Emotional, introspective mood, urban storytelling photography, DSLR quality, 85mm lens look, f/1.8, high dynamic range, 8K, professional color grading.
```

## Примечания
Усиленная версия:

```text
Ultra-realistic cinematic street portrait of the person from the uploaded image standing perfectly still in the middle of a crowded city street, looking directly into the camera with a calm, intense expression. His face is in razor-sharp focus while people rush around him in a long-exposure blur, creating dynamic directional motion streaks. The subject remains crisp and motionless, isolated against the flowing crowd. Natural soft daylight, shallow depth of field, creamy bokeh, realistic skin texture, subtle freckles, neutral makeup, dark winter coat and scarf. Moody, emotional, introspective atmosphere, urban storytelling photography, DSLR quality, 85mm lens, f/1.8, high dynamic range, 8K, professional cinematic color grading.
```

Negative prompt:

```text
blurry face, distorted eyes, extra fingers, duplicated people, bad anatomy, low detail, plastic skin, oversaturated colors, cartoon, painting, CGI, fake motion blur, noisy image, warped background, deformed hands, out of frame, low quality
```
