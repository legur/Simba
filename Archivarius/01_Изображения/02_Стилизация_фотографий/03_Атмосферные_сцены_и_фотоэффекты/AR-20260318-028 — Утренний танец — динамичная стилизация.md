## Инструкция
1. Загрузи reference photo человека или пары.
2. Вставь основной промпт ниже.
3. При необходимости отдельно добавь aspect ratio или negative prompt, не меняя текст основного промпта.

Using the uploaded reference photo as the ONLY face and identity reference for the dancing couple, preserve recognizable facial features and identity, conceptual objects, holographic microcircuits, random landscape space scenes combined with dancing couple figurines made of precious stones and metals, paper filigree flowers, sun flare glare
