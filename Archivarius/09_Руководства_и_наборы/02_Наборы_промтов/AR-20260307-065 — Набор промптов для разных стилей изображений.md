## Пост
Набор готовых стилевых промтов для генерации изображений по фото человека.
Подходит как база для image-to-image задач, когда нужно быстро примерить разные визуальные миры и художественные стили.

## Инструкция
1. Open ChatGPT
2. Upload Your Image
3. Выберите один из промтов ниже
4. При необходимости добавьте требование сохранить внешность 1:1

## Промпты

### Prompt 1 - Pixar 3D Animation
```text
Transform the person in the photo into a 3D Pixar-style character. High-quality render, expressive large eyes, smooth skin textures, soft cinematic lighting, and a vibrant, whimsical background.
```

### Prompt 2 - Classic Disney (Hand-drawn)
```text
Convert this person into a classic 1990s Disney hand-drawn animation style. Clean line art, soft cel-shading, friendly facial features, and a magical, storybook aesthetic.
```

### Prompt 3 - Studio Ghibli (Anime)
```text
Reimagine the person in the style of Studio Ghibli. Hand-painted watercolor background, simple yet expressive facial features, peaceful atmosphere, high attention to detail in clothing and nature.
```

### Prompt 4 - Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse
```text
Transform this person into a character from Spider-Verse. Stylized comic book aesthetic, halftone dot patterns, vibrant chromatic aberration, and dynamic street-art background.
```

### Prompt 5 - South Park
```text
A 2D cutout character in the South Park style based on this photo. Simple shapes, construction paper texture, oversized head, and iconic winter gear, standing in a snowy mountain town.
```

### Prompt 6 - Cyberpunk / Futuristic Anime
```text
A high-tech cyberpunk anime version of the person in the photo. Neon lighting reflections, cybernetic details, futuristic streetwear, sharp linework, and a rainy Tokyo-inspired background.
```

### Prompt 7 - LEGO Minifigure
```text
Convert the person into a realistic LEGO minifigure. Plastic glossy texture, iconic yellow head shape (or realistic skin tone), LEGO-style hairpiece, and a background made of building blocks.
```

### Prompt 8 - Tim Burton Style
```text
A gothic, whimsical character based on the photo in the style of Tim Burton. Pale skin, large sunken eyes, spindly limbs, dark curly hair, and a moody, monochromatic Victorian-esque setting.
```

### Prompt 9 - Grand Theft Auto (GTA V) Loading Screen
```text
Digital illustration of the person in the style of GTA V loading screen art. Heavy saturation, high contrast, thick artistic brushstrokes, bold outlines, and a stylized California background.
```

## Примечания
• Это prompt_pack, а не одиночный промт.
• Лучше всего использовать с фото лица или портретом по пояс.
• Для более точного результата можно добавлять:
  • preserve identity
  • keep facial features
  • same hairstyle
  • same clothing colors
• Некоторые стили могут конфликтовать с коммерческими IP по узнаваемости, поэтому иногда лучше использовать формулировку “inspired by”.
